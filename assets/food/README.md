# VisiBox food illustrations

`raphael-atlas.png` is an original AI-generated, transparent 4 × 4 atlas created for VisiBox. The art direction is Raphael-inspired Renaissance still life: painted forms, warm natural colors, and consistent lighting. No platform emoji are used to render food.

Cells, left to right, top to bottom: strawberries, apple, carrots, broccoli; milk, eggs, cheese, yogurt; chicken, steak, fish, leftovers; bread, sauce, rice, tomatoes. `FoodIcon.tsx` clips the bundled atlas without platform-specific image processing. Unknown foods use their category illustration, rather than inventing an exact image.
